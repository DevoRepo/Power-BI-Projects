# Power BI Projects

Business-intelligence dashboards built in Power BI Desktop. Each one starts from raw data, goes through ETL in Power Query and a relational data model, and ends in DAX measures and visuals that answer specific business questions.

## 1. Reliance Smart: Sales Performance Report

File: `Reliance.pbix`

- **KPI cards** compare revenue and profit with their monthly goals. In the snapshot above, revenue is 120.16K against a 113.79K goal (+5.6 %) and profit is 71.68K against 67.87K (+5.61 %).
- **Brand-wise table** shows transactions, units sold, revenue and profit for every product brand, across about 2.7 lakh transactions.
- **Region-wise revenue map** and a **state-level treemap** show where sales come from.
- **Under the hood:** data cleaned and transformed in Power Query, a relational model linking sales, products and regions, and DAX measures for profit and growth rate.

## 2. Bank Customer Churn Analysis

File: `Churn Analysis Of Bank.pbix`

A dashboard that looks at which bank customers leave and how churn varies across customer segments, as a starting point for retention decisions.

## 3. Tata Group Data Visualisation

File: `TATA FORAGE.pbix`

Built for the Tata Group *Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights* job simulation on Forage (certificate earned January 2025). The visuals were chosen to answer leadership questions about business performance.
